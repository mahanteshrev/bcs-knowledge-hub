Know Sigmoid

What Sigmoid does as a company, and where BCS fits within it.

The Business

Know BCS

What BCS does, and how it's led.

Business Consulting Services (BCS) is Sigmoid's consulting practice — the layer that brings together business context, consulting thinking, domain knowledge, analytics and technology to solve client problems and create measurable business value. BCS sits inside the Data Science pillar and is led by Shashikiran PB, Vice President, with a team of Senior Directors, Directors and Associate Directors each owning a portfolio of client accounts.

BCS's client work is organized into portfolios. Each portfolio groups together a handful of client accounts under a single Portfolio Lead, who owns the relationship, the delivery quality and the growth of that portfolio.
